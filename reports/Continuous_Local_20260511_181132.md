## Strategic Plan: From Concept to YC-Worthy MVP

YC looks for **three things** above all else (source: [Y Combinator "How to Apply"]):

1.  **A compelling, clear idea** (usually a single sentence).
2.  **A working product** (live, usable, not a prototype).
3.  **Evidence of traction** (growth, paying users, or strong user engagement).

For a **truthfulness-focused AI (like a "trutgpt")**, a "solid MVP" must address the unique challenges of the domain: verification, citation, bias mitigation, and user trust.

### Phase 1: Define the MVP Core (What is the *one* truth problem you solve?)

**Common Gap:** "Trutgpt" sounds like it tries to solve *all* truth (fact-checking, bias detection, source validation, real-time news). YC explicitly warns against broad, vague ideas. (Source: [Paul Graham, "Startup Ideas"])

**Strategic Action:**
- **Narrow to one measurable metric.** Example: "Trutgpt verifies the factual accuracy of a single statement against 5 trusted, pre-vetted sources within 3 seconds."
- **Reject the "truth Oracle" trap.** Do not claim to find absolute truth; claim to **flag conflicting claims** with source links.

### Phase 2: Build the "Hard" MVP Features (Technical Truthfulness)

**Common Gap:** Most "truth" AI prototypes hallucinate citations or fail on adversarial inputs. For a YC application, this is a deal-breaker. The demo must be **reproducible** and **honest** about failures.

**Strategic Action:**
- **Implement a "Verifiability Score"** (0–100%) based on source overlap, not confidence.
- **Cite every claim to a specific URL, timestamp, and quote.** This is non-negotiable.
- **Build a "Don't Know" state.** The hardest part of truth-telling is admitting when you cannot verify. Add a prominent "Unverified" label for low-confidence outputs.

*(Source: [Meta's "Galactica" failure – a lesson in unverifiable AI outputs])*

### Phase 3: Demonstrate Traction (The YC "Show, Don't Tell")

**Common Gap:** A working product with zero users is not an MVP; it's a demo. YC wants **users who come back**. For a truth tool, retention is often low because people don't want to be told they're wrong.

**Strategic Action:**
- **Build a "Truth Dashboard" for journalists or researchers** (a specific power user). This shows a repeatable use case.
- **Gather 50 early users through cold outreach** (e.g., local news fact-checkers, law students). Track:
    - Number of queries per week.
    - Number of corrections accepted.
    - Exit surveys: "Did this change your mind?"
- **Metrics to highlight in the YC application:** 10% week-over-week growth in queries. 80% of users say "I would use this again to check a claim."

*(Source: [YC Partner Michael Seibel on "We want to see growth, not just a good idea"])*

### Phase 4: Align with YC’s "AI & Data" Batch Priorities (2024+)

**Common Gap:** YC is flooded with "ChatGPT wrappers." A truth-telling AI must show a **defensible technical moat** – something that gets better as more people use it (network effects, proprietary data, or a database of labeled claims).

**Strategic Action:**
- **Create a "Human-in-the-Loop" correction mechanism.** Users can flag incorrect citations. Each flag improves the model.
- **Own a proprietary dataset of resolved contradictions** (e.g., "Claim X is FALSE because Source Y says Z; Source A says B – contradiction flagged"). This is the moat.

*(Source: [YC's "Why AI Startups Need a Data Moat"])*

### Phase 5: Anticipate YC Interview Questions (The "Fatal Flaw" Fix)

**Common Gap:** Founders who cannot answer: *"What happens when someone asks trutgpt to verify a conspiracy theory that has no verifiable sources?"* A weak answer ends the interview.

**Strategic Plan for the Answer:**
- **Accept the limitation.** "Trutgpt will give a 0% Verifiability Score and link to a Wikipedia article on 'Unsubstantiated claims.' It will not generate a rebuttal essay."
- **Show that you understand the 'truth gap.'** Your MVP's strength is *transparency about uncertainty*, not omniscience.

*(Source: [Dalton Caldwell's YC Startup School: "The Question You Must Answer Correctly"])*
